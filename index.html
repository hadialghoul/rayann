From Hadi

For Rayan

Dear Rayan,

This letter is only for you, my sister. Slow down for a moment and stay here with me.

You are so good, Rayan.

You are gentle, and you are full of light. Let your shoulders drop. Let your heart rest. There is nothing you have to fix right now.

Be calm. Breathe.

I can feel you, Rayan. I understand you.

I will be with you. Not later, and not from far away. Right here, beside you. You are not alone.

I am proud of you. Whenever you need a soft place, come back to this letter. I meant every word.

Hadi

P.S. Be calm, Rayan. I am with you.
